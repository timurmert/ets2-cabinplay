CabinPlay - a working media screen for your truck cabin (Euro Truck Simulator 2)

INSTALL
  1. Close the game.
  2. Double-click Install.cmd.
     If Windows says it protected your PC, click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     (The files are not code-signed.)
  3. Start the game and accept the "SDK features" notice it shows once.
  4. In the Mod Manager, switch on "CabinPlay Screen".
  5. In a truck workshop, fit "CabinPlay Screen" in the left windscreen accessory slot
     (Large or Compact).

USE
  Ctrl+Alt+C   use the screen from inside the game with mouse and keyboard (Esc to leave)
  Ctrl+Alt+N   navigation map          Ctrl+Alt+H   home screen
  Ctrl+Alt+Space  play / pause         Ctrl+Alt+F   video fullscreen
  The language and all shortcuts are under Settings on the screen.

NEEDS
  Euro Truck Simulator 2 1.61, Windows 10 or 11, the Cabin Accessories DLC.
  Single player only: it does not work with TruckersMP.

HELP
  Open Settings on the screen, press "Create error report" and share the zip it saves
  on our Discord.

REMOVE
  Double-click Uninstall.cmd.
